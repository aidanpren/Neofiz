Why the tube is drawn twice

The solver works on a half-plane and revolves it, so one wall is computed and the other is its mirror. The dashed line down the middle is the axis of revolution; the dotted outline is where the tube started, at rest, before any pressure.

The scrub track is the measurement

It plots the pressure the wall could hold at each instant, read off an exact equilibrium identity rather than from the pump. That is why it is still defined after the peak, where there is no equilibrium at all and the applied pressure has run away from what the tube can carry.

There is no crack, and that is the finding

Every element carries its own failure strain drawn from a correlated random field, so this tube has a worst ring somewhere and no two seeds agree where. But it does not neck: it is still swelling almost evenly as it gives way, and by the last frame the whole wall is damaged rather than one station of it. A shell shares load along its axis, so it averages its flaws instead of failing at the worst one.
